DCF Analysis

How is this calculated? Exit Multiple DCF Method:

1. We project the company's financial metric (EBITDA/Earnings) into the future using your Growth Rate.
2. We multiply the final projected metric by your Terminal Multiple to get a Future Stock Price.
3. We discount that Future Price back to today's dollars using your Desired Return rate to find the Fair Value.
